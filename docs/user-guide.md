# DDU Mirror — User Guide

## What it is

DDU Mirror shows what a hostile party could learn from your public footprint, then lets you approve every expansion before it runs.

## Mock vs Real

- **Mock** — synthetic findings for demos. No live discovery.
- **Real** — onboarding wizard collects identifiers you control, then drafts proposals against those only.

## Approvals

Nothing irreversible-looking runs without you. Approve, reject, or expand one hop from the proposal queue.

## 3D scene

Height and glow map to severity. Depth is hop distance from your seed identity. Pink edges are active attack paths. If WebGL is unavailable, a text hierarchy is shown instead.

## Voice and vision

Voice answers are grounded in non-rejected findings and scrubbed of PII. Vision analyzes photos you upload (10MB max); demo assets are pre-cached.

## Wipe

**End & wipe** permanently destroys findings, vault values, and attack paths for the session. Use it when you are done.
