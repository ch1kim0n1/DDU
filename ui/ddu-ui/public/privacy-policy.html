DDU Mirror Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

What we collect

Identifiers you provide during onboarding: email addresses, usernames/handles, and optional profile URLs. Optional context such as state/county and a free-text guess about your exposure. Session activity (approvals, rejections, wipes) is recorded in an append-only action log for that session only.

How we use it

Data is used solely to discover and display your own public footprint and to propose human-approved expansions. We do not sell personal data. Discovery refuses targets outside the identifiers you verified.

How long it is stored

Sessions are ephemeral. Findings and raw evidence values are destroyed when you wipe the session, when the session TTL expires (default 30 minutes), or when the process restarts if Redis is not configured. Append-only audit logs may retain redacted action metadata for compliance until the retention window elapses. Nothing is retained as a long-term marketing profile. You can export your current session via "Download my data" while it exists.

Third-party processors (optional)

  • ElevenLabs — only if configured; used for optional speech-to-text / TTS. Transcripts are scrubbed of PII before LLM use.
  • LLM provider (e.g. Anthropic) — only if configured; receives scrubbed, allowlisted fields for remediation copy and grounded answers.
  • Map tile providers — MapLibre may load OpenStreetMap tiles in your browser; exact GPS values stay redacted server-side by default.

Your rights

You may wipe your session at any time ("End & wipe"), which permanently destroys findings, attack paths, and vault entries for that session. You may also request an export of the current session data while it exists.

Biometric data

Liveness checks run locally in your browser (MediaPipe). Face mesh processing does not stream video to our servers. At most one verified frame may be uploaded after you pass, and biometric templates are short-lived and destroyed with the session or when you close the panel.
